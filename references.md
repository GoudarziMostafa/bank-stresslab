# Methodological references

- Rüdiger Frey and Alexander J. McNeil (2002), *VaR and expected shortfall in portfolios of dependent credit risks: Conceptual and practical insights*, Journal of Banking & Finance. Motivates the comparison of default models calibrated to the same marginal default probability and default correlation. [Author manuscript](https://statmath.wu.ac.at/~frey/publications/rome.pdf) · [University of York publication record](https://pure.york.ac.uk/portal/en/publications/var-and-expected-shortfall-in-portfolios-of-dependent-credit-risk/)
- Rüdiger Frey and Alexander J. McNeil (2003), *Dependent defaults in models of portfolio credit risk*, Journal of Risk. Example 4.8 compares mixing distributions with matched first two moments. Example 4.11 develops the conditional independence representation for normal mixtures. [Author manuscript](https://www.wu.ac.at/fileadmin/wu/d/i/statmath/Publications/Publications_Frey/Journal_Papers/frey-mcneil-JoR.pdf)
- Carlo Acerbi and Dirk Tasche (2002), *On the coherence of expected shortfall*, Journal of Banking & Finance. Establishes the quantile-integral formulation used to account for probability mass at VaR. [Author manuscript](https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/0104295)
- Darrell Duffie, Andreas Eckner, Guillaume Horel and Leandro Saita (2009), *Frailty Correlated Default*, Journal of Finance. Figures 4–5 distinguish uncertainty about the current common credit environment from future shared shocks. This motivates the dynamic experiment with an illustrative stationary Gaussian factor. [Author manuscript](https://web.stanford.edu/~duffie/fcdt.pdf)
- GEMs Consortium (2024), *Default and recovery statistics: Private and public lending 1994–2023*. The empirical comparison uses the private counterparty counts in Table 5 and the average private contract recovery in Table 2. The original portfolio and the dynamic validation experiments use illustrative inputs. [EIB publication and source report](https://www.eib.org/en/publications/20240217-default-and-recovery-statistics-private-and-public-lending-1994-2023)
- Basel Committee on Banking Supervision. The capital interpretation uses the corporate correlation curve and the foundation LGD rules. [CRE31](https://www.bis.org/basel_framework/chapter/CRE/31.htm?inforce=20230101&published=20200327) · [CRE32](https://www.bis.org/basel_framework/chapter/CRE/32.htm?inforce=20230101&published=20200327) · [IRB explanatory note](https://www.bis.org/bcbs/irbriskweight.htm)
